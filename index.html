What picture is your marriage painting?

01Overview

Every marriage paints pictures.

The question is, what kind of picture are you painting as husband and wife?

From Genesis to Revelation, God gives us vivid pictures of what marriage was meant to be. They are more than metaphors. They carry sacred truths that shape how husbands and wives love, speak to, and live with one another. First and foremost, marriage is a picture of Christ’s love for His church, and of the church’s glad respect for her Savior (Ephesians 5:31-32).

Many of us came to the altar with a different picture in mind. Years later, some couples feel let down by unmet expectations. Others are simply worn down by the ordinary friction of life. The good news is that God, by His Word and Spirit, teaches us to paint well. As Scott Brown writes, “There is hope because you still have the canvas before you.”

Drawn from Scott’s book Getting the Picture Right in Your Marriage, this conference walks through those biblical pictures in short, practical sessions:

  1. the mission God gives to wives and to husbands
  2. how to have the hard conversations
  3. how not to be hard to live with
  4. the aroma of a Christ-filled home
  5. how to correct your spouse in love, and when to let love cover
  6. keeping intimacy alive
  7. real hope for every marriage, whatever condition it is in

Providence Baptist Church and Gloria Deo Baptist Church are partnering to host this weekend. It is open to the whole family, not only married couples. Engaged and single adults who hope to marry one day are welcome. Children attend with their parents, and there is a dedicated children’s breakout Saturday afternoon. The weekend closes with a joint Lord’s Day worship service, with Scott preaching, and a fellowship potluck.

Come ready to sing, to listen, to laugh, and to go home and paint a more beautiful picture.

03Sessions

Eleven sessions.

All sessions by Scott Brown unless noted.

01
Friday · 6:30–7:30 PM · 60 min

Getting the Picture Right

Most of us married with a picture already in mind, and many have since felt let down. God gives a better picture. Marriage is a representative relationship: an earthly union shaped by a greater one, meant to show forth Christ’s love for His church and the church’s love for her Savior (Ephesians 5:22-33). This opening session frames the whole weekend: your marriage is a canvas, and God teaches you how to paint.

02
Saturday · 9:15–9:35 AM

The Mission of a Wife

The happiest and most settled women are those who know who they are and gladly take their place in God’s order. This session looks at a wife’s calling to help, honor, and respect her husband “as to the Lord,” and at the peace, confidence, and beauty that follow when she does.

03
Saturday · 9:35–9:55 AM

The Mission of a Husband

A husband is called to love his wife as Christ loved the church: to lead, nourish, cherish, wash, and sacrifice for her. This session also takes up a simple charge Scott learned from his father, who said it to dozens of men: “Son, hug your honey.” Pay attention. Spend time with her. Don’t be so busy.

04
Saturday · 9:55–10:15 AM

How to Have Tough Conversations

Every marriage has hard things to talk about. This session shows how to speak the truth in love, choose the right time and tone, and walk through difficult conversations in a way that builds oneness instead of wearing it down.

05
Saturday · 10:30–10:50 AM

Don’t Be Hard to Live With

“What is it like to live with you?” Some fabrics are scratchy and some are smooth. Some roads wear a car down. Drawing on Galatians 5, this session asks whether your spouse is lifted up or worn down by living with you, and shows that walking in the Spirit makes marriage easy, not hard.

06
Saturday · 10:50–11:10 AM

The Aroma of Your Home

Every home gives off an aroma: a feel, an atmosphere. Some homes carry the fragrance of Christ (2 Corinthians 2:15). Others carry the relational equivalent of spoiled food. This session shows how the fruit of the Spirit fills a home with an aroma of blessing that children, guests, and spouses sense the moment they walk in.

07
Saturday · 11:20–11:40 AM

How to Make Marriage Easier

God wants to see joy in marriage. From the first outburst of joy in Eden to the law that gave a new husband a year at home to “bring happiness to his wife” (Deuteronomy 24:5), Scripture shows a joyful couple. This session gives practical patterns and habits that take the friction out of daily life together.

08
Saturday · 11:40 AM–12:00 PM

How to Correct Your Spouse

One of the most beautiful pictures of marriage is a husband washing his wife with the water of the Word (Ephesians 5:26), with husband and wife helping one another grow in holiness. This session shows how to give and receive correction with humility and gentleness, so that correction cleanses instead of wounds.

09
Saturday · 2:10–2:30 PM

You Don’t Need to Fix Everything

When couples try to fix their marriage, they often go on the warpath: making lists, tracking infractions, and spraying the “-cides” on one another until love, joy, and peace are gone. “Love will cover a multitude of sins” (1 Peter 4:8). This session teaches the grace of patiently enduring and covering imperfections.

10
Saturday · 2:30–2:50 PM

Don’t Let Sexual Intimacy Die

“Do not deprive one another” (1 Corinthians 7:5). The one-flesh union is a picture of Christ’s love for His bride, with its joy, tenderness, security, and faithfulness. With pastoral care and discretion, this session addresses how couples can guard and renew intimacy through every season of marriage.

For married and engaged adults. Younger children are still in the children’s breakout, which runs until 3:05 PM.

11
Saturday · 2:50–3:10 PM

Hope for Your Marriage

What if the picture you have painted for years is distorted? It is not over yet. God is a restorer and a healer, and He blesses those who repent and turn. “There is hope because you still have the canvas before you.” This closing session sends couples home to paint over the ugliness with His beauty, “not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit” (Zechariah 4:6).

05Speakers

Scott and Deborah Brown.

SB

Scott T. Brown

Scott T. Brown is the president of Church and Family Life and pastor at Hope Baptist Church in Wake Forest, North Carolina. Scott graduated from California State University in Fullerton with a degree in History and received a Master of Divinity degree from Talbot School of Theology. He gives most of his time to local pastoral ministry, expository preaching, and conferences on church and family reformation. Scott helps people think through the two greatest institutions God has provided: the church and the family. He is the author of Getting the Picture Right in Your Marriage.

DB

Deborah Brown

Deborah Brown is the wife of Scott Brown. She is a happy mother of four children who have so far given her twenty-nine grandchildren. She is a Proverbs 31 woman and dairy farmer. Deborah is known far and wide for her hospitality and her practical, God-centered, no-compromise counsel.
